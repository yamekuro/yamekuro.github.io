01 / ABOUT

The other side of the alert.

Every alert has a system behind it. Five years in IT infrastructure, in a bank's data centre, on the help desk and in consultancy, taught me how those systems behave when nothing is wrong. Now I use that to build detections from real telemetry, test them with controlled attacks and publish what held up and what didn't.

THE APPROACH

Understand the system.
Then investigate the signal.

Having worked in infrastructure at Grupo Santander and in technology consultancy, I know that context matters. An alert is more than a collection of fields.

My labs are where I build systems, attack them under control and check which detections actually hold up. Every result is written up, including what did not work.

FEATURED PROJECT / SECURITY OPERATIONS

soc-homelab

A segmented SOC lab on a single workstation: an OPNsense firewall between six zones, Wazuh with Microsoft Sentinel and Splunk, phishing analysis, vulnerability management and an AI assistant that has to pass an evaluation before it acts.

It is designed against a catalogue of eight ATT&CK scenarios, from brute force to credential theft in Active Directory, and every phase has an exit gate that must be verified before the next one starts. A parallel cloud track covers LLMjacking in AWS.
